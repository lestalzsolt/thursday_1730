Player table

Ranked by average event points per day attended. Each Thursday gives points for where your team finished: with 2 teams gold 3, silver 2; with 3 teams gold 3, silver 2, bronze 1; with 4 teams gold 4, silver 3, bronze 2, 4th 1. The game stats (W, D, L, points per game) count 3 for a win and 1 for a draw in each game. "+/-" is your team's goal difference in the games you played. By default the table shows players who came to at least half of the Thursdays.
